Turn any home into an escape room.

How it works

Hide. Scan. Escape.

EscapOtap gives you the story, the puzzles, the clock and the clues. You bring the house.

Pick a room

Start with the 5-minute tutorial, then try a haunted manor, a bank heist, a lab outbreak, a pirate hunt or a murder mystery.

Hide the clues

Write cheap NFC stickers in the app, or print QR clue cards. Each room tells you exactly where every clue goes.

Play

Players scan clues with their iPhones to find items, open locks and crack puzzles before time runs out.

See it

Mission control meets mystery box

Features

Everything for game night

👑

Be the Game Master

One iPhone runs the game while up to 8 players join from their own phones. Watch every team live, answer hint requests, add time and trigger blackouts. No internet or accounts needed.

🔍

Play solo

No Game Master? Any room plays on a single iPhone, with automatic hints when you get stuck.

🧩

21 puzzle types

Code locks, safe dials, padlocks, ciphers with decoder wheels, Morse by flashlight, logic grids, tilt mazes, jigsaws, hidden audio, and puzzles that need several players at once.

🛠️

Room Builder

Make your own escape room: write the story, add puzzles, link them into a flow, photograph each hiding spot, and check it can be solved before you hide a single tag.

🖨️

Print Kit

Print QR clue cards, a Game Master cheat sheet, cipher wheels and logic grid handouts on Letter or A4.

🏆

Hall of Fame

Best times, run history, awards, optional Game Center leaderboards, and a result card to share.

Pricing

Free to start

The tutorial, The Haunted Manor and the Room Builder (two rooms of your own) are free. Only the Game Master needs to own a room; players always join free.

Theme packs

Unlock any other built-in room on its own, once, for good.

EscapOtap Pro

Every room, unlimited rooms of your own, and every puzzle type in the Room Builder. Yearly, or a one-time lifetime purchase.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your rooms and games stay on your devices. Read the privacy policy.

What you need

An iPhone and a sense of adventure

iPhone

Any iPhone running iOS 18 or later. Scanning and writing NFC tags uses the iPhone’s built-in NFC reader.

NFC stickers (optional)

NTAG215 stickers are recommended. NTAG213 and NTAG216 work too. Packs of 20–50 are inexpensive online.

Or a printer

No stickers? Print QR clue cards from the Print Kit and scan them with the camera instead.
