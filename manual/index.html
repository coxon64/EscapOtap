1. Welcome

EscapOtap turns any home into an escape room. Each room is a story with puzzles, items, locks and a ticking clock. You hide physical clues around the house: NFC stickers or printed QR cards. Players find them and scan them with an iPhone to reveal clues, pick up items, open locks and unlock puzzles, until they escape or time runs out.

There are three ways to play:

  • Solo Mode: one iPhone runs everything, with automatic hints. Great for one player or a group sharing a phone.
  • Host a Game: you’re the Game Master. Your iPhone runs the clock and the control room, and up to 8 players join on their own iPhones, in up to 4 competing teams.
  • Join a Game: connect to a nearby Game Master and play.

2. What you need

  • An iPhone with iOS 18 or later. Every iPhone that runs iOS 18 can read and write NFC tags.
  • Clues to hide, either:
    • NFC stickers. NTAG215 is recommended. NTAG213 and NTAG216 work too, as does MIFARE Ultralight. Use “anti-metal” tags on metal surfaces. MIFARE Classic tags don’t work with iPhone.
    • QR cards, printed from the Print Kit. They work on any iPhone and need no stickers.
  • For hosted games: each player needs EscapOtap on their own iPhone, with Wi-Fi and Bluetooth turned on. No internet connection or shared Wi-Fi network is needed; phones connect directly.

Try it with nothing at all: open any room in the Rooms Library and choose ⋯ › Playtest. You can “scan” every clue from an on-screen list.

3. First launch

The first time you open EscapOtap, six short pages explain the idea, what you need and your privacy. Swipe through them or tap Skip.

The last page explains each permission before iOS asks for it. You can allow the camera there. The other permissions are asked for the first time you use the feature that needs them:

  • Camera: scanning QR clue cards and join codes, and photographing hiding spots.
  • Local Network and Bluetooth: connecting phones in hosted games.
  • Nearby Interaction: measuring distance for proximity puzzles.
  • Microphone: recording voice clues in the Room Builder.

NFC needs no permission. Finish with Play the Tutorial to jump straight into the 5-minute tutorial room, The Locked Study.

4. The Home screen

Play

  • Host a Game: be the Game Master for players on other iPhones.
  • Join a Game: join a nearby Game Master.
  • Solo Mode: play on this iPhone alone.

Explore

  • Rooms Library: every room on your iPhone, with records and setup tools.
  • Room Builder: make your own rooms.
  • Tag Workshop: write, check and erase NFC tags.
  • Print Kit: printable clue cards, cheat sheets and handouts.
  • Hall of Fame: your records, history and awards.

The gear button opens Settings. If you leave a hosted game partway through, a You’re in a Game card appears on Home with a Rejoin button.

5. Setting up a room

Each room needs its clues hidden before you play. Every clue has a label saying where it goes, such as “Under the couch”.

  1. Choose a room. Start with The Locked Study: it uses just 3 clues.
  2. Make the clues. Either:
    • Open Tag Workshop, pick the room and tap Batch Write. Hold each blank sticker to the top of your iPhone when asked: “Tag 4 of 12: Kitchen drawer”. Or
    • Open Print Kit, pick the room and print the Clue cards.
  3. Hide them. Put each one where its label says. Writing the label on the back of a sticker in pencil helps when you reset the room.
  4. Check them. In the Tag Workshop, Health Check lets you scan every tag in a row and lists any that are missing.

Game Masters: print the GM cheat sheet from the Print Kit. It has the full walkthrough, every answer, where each clue goes and a reset checklist. Keep it away from players!

Tags are reusable. Rewrite a sticker for another room at any time; the old room stops recognising it.

6. Playing solo

  1. Home › Solo Mode › pick a room. The room screen shows its story, length, player count, rules and the list of clues to hide.
  2. Choose the time limit and whether the clock counts down or up, then tap Start Game.
  3. The story plays with typewriter text (tap to finish it, or tap Read Aloud). Tap Begin to start the clock.
  4. Search the room, tap SCAN and hold the top of your iPhone to a clue. Solve puzzles as they appear.
  5. Escape before the clock runs out. The end screen shows your time, score, any new awards, and lets you rate the room and share a result card.

Hints: tap the lightbulb. Each puzzle has three hints, from a gentle nudge to a big push. They cost time or points depending on Settings › Game, and there’s a short wait between hints. In Solo Mode, a hint is also offered automatically if you’re stuck on the same puzzle for a while.

Pausing and saving: pause from the menu button. If you leave the app or it closes, your game is saved; Solo Mode then shows a Continue card that resumes it, paused, at the moment it was saved. Quitting from the pause menu ends the game without recording it.

7. The game screen

The same screen is used in Solo Mode and by players in hosted games.

  • Timer: time left (or elapsed). Turn on Large timer in Settings › Display for a bigger clock. Warnings sound at 5 minutes, 1 minute and for the final 10 seconds.
  • Objective: what to focus on next.
  • Puzzle cards: puzzles you can work on now. Tap one to open it.
  • SCAN: the big button. Hold the top edge of your iPhone to a clue.
  • QR: scan a printed QR clue card with the camera instead.
  • Inventory: items you’ve found. Tap an item to inspect it: drag to turn it over, double-tap to spin it. Some items reveal details only when inspected. Drag items together, or into a puzzle’s slots, to combine them.
  • Journal: every clue you’ve found, plus private Notes and a Sketch pad with pen, marker and eraser. Journal pages stay on your iPhone.
  • Hints: the lightbulb (see above). In hosted games you may need to Ask the Game Master.
  • Team chat (hosted games): quick messages like “Over here!” or short typed notes to your team. Messages from the Game Master show a megaphone.

What a scan can reveal

  • Clue: information, saved in the journal.
  • Item: added to your inventory.
  • Lock: opens if you’ve solved what it needs or hold the right item; otherwise it tells you what’s missing, like “This lock needs the Brass Key”.
  • Puzzle: opens a puzzle to solve.
  • Event: something happens: a story moment, a sound, or a surprise.
  • Red herring: a fake-out. Not everything is a clue!
  • The exit: escapes the room once everything it needs is solved.

Wrong answers may cost time, depending on Settings › Game › Wrong answer.

Scanning from the Lock Screen: during a game you can hold a locked iPhone to a tag. Tap the notification that appears, and the scan goes straight into your game.

8. Hosting a game (Game Master)

The Game Master’s iPhone runs the official game: the clock, every team’s progress and all the rules. Players’ phones connect to it.

  1. Make sure the room’s clues are hidden (see Setting up a room).
  2. Home › Host a Game › pick a room. Only the Game Master needs to own the room.
  3. Choose the time limit, clock direction and teams (1 to 4). Options include Allow late joining, Players choose their team, and Approve hint requests (players ask you for hints instead of getting them automatically).
  4. Tap Open Lobby. A 4-digit code and a QR code appear. Allow Local Network access when asked.
  5. As players join, they appear in the lobby with a connection dot and latency. Rename teams and move players between them, or let players choose.
  6. Tap Start Game. Every player sees the story, then the game screen with your clock.

Your screen stays awake while hosting. Don’t lock your phone or switch apps during a game: iOS pauses the connection while EscapOtap is in the background.

If your app closes mid-game, reopen it and tap Host a Game › Resume. The game comes back paused and players reconnect.

9. The control room

Once the game starts, the Game Master’s screen becomes a control room. In portrait, the clock is at the top with tabs below. Turn the iPhone sideways for a split view: clock and teams on the left, everything else on the right.

Clock

Pause and resume every phone at once, add or remove a minute, or open the Time menu for ±5 minutes, per-team time, and Walls Closing In (the clock runs at double speed for 30 seconds). End finishes the game early, with or without declaring a winner.

Teams

A card for each team: progress, what they’re working on, their last scan (“2m ago: Ada scanned the Kitchen Drawer”), hints used, inventory, and each player’s connection. Use a player’s menu to move them to another team or remove them.

Hints

When Approve hint requests is on, requests appear here with a sound and a badge. For each one you can:

  • Send the next written hint (you see a preview).
  • Write your own hint, shown to players as “From the Game Master”.
  • Not Yet: decline, with an optional note.

Choose whether hints you send cost the usual time or points, and send unprompted hints to any team.

Control

Pick a target (everyone or one team), then send a message, or trigger an event: blackout, alarm, ghost whisper, walls closing in, unlock a puzzle, or a bonus of points or time. You can also send a story message and fire the room’s own scripted events.

Sound

A soundboard that plays effects on players’ phones (and optionally yours), and switches the background music between the room’s own, other themes, or silence.

Map

Each team’s puzzle flow as a diagram, coloured solved, open or locked. Tap a locked puzzle to unlock it for that team.

Feed

A live log of every scan, solve, wrong answer, hint and chat message, filterable by team.

Results

When the game ends, your results rank the teams (a declared winner comes first), and players see their team’s MVP awards, such as Most Scans, Code Cracker and Eagle Eye. Tap Close Game when you’re done.

10. Joining a game

  1. Turn on Wi-Fi and Bluetooth. You don’t need to join any network.
  2. Home › Join a Game. Allow Local Network access when asked.
  3. Join by typing the Game Master’s 4-digit code, tapping their game under Nearby Games and entering the code, or scanning their QR code with the iPhone Camera app.
  4. Wait in the lobby. If the Game Master allows it, tap Join Team to pick your team.

Set your name and avatar first in Settings › Player Profile. Players never need to buy anything.

If you lose connection, a banner says EscapOtap is reconnecting, and it reconnects by itself when it can. If the app closed, reopen it and tap Rejoin on Home. Pausing belongs to the Game Master: when they pause, you see “Paused by the Game Master”.

Multiplayer puzzles: in hosted games, Split Clue shows each teammate a different part of a clue, Simultaneous Scan needs different players scanning different tags at the same moment, and Proximity Hunt has you track down the Game Master’s phone.

11. Puzzle types

EscapOtap has 21 kinds of puzzle. Answers are forgiving: capital letters, spaces and small typos usually don’t matter.

PuzzleHow it works
Code LockEnter a number on a keypad, or turn a safe dial and tap Set for each number.
Combination PadlockSpin the number wheels (swipe, or tap the top or bottom of a wheel).
Word LockSpin letter wheels to spell the word.
RiddleType the answer.
Multiple ChoicePick the right answer or answers.
Logic GridUse the clues to work out who goes with what. Tap clues to cross them off, and mark ✓ and ✗ in the grid; a ✓ fills its row and column with ✗.
CipherDecode a secret message: Caesar (with a decoder wheel), Atbash, Pigpen (with its key) or substitution (with a workings grid).
MorseA message flashed by the flashlight, the screen, or vibrations. Slow replays it slower. Write down the dots and dashes, then decode them.
Audio ClueListen to a recording. Some are backwards (try Reverse), slowed down, or hide a tone.
Image InspectPinch to zoom and drag to pan a picture, and tap the hidden details.
Jigsaw FragmentsScan each fragment’s tag, then assemble the picture by dragging or tapping pieces into place.
Pattern LockDraw the right shape across a 3×3 grid of dots (or tap the dots in order).
Tilt MazeTilt your iPhone to roll the ball to the key, avoiding the holes. Arrow buttons are available instead.
Timed ChallengeTap Start, then answer every round before the countdown ends, or take a penalty and try again.
Scan SequenceScan a set of tags in the right order. A wrong tag starts the sequence over.
Item UseScan a lock while holding the right item.
Item CombineDrag the right items into the slots to make something new.
Simultaneous ScanScan several tags within a few seconds of each other. In hosted games, different players must each scan one. Solo players get extra time.
Proximity HuntA hot-and-cold radar leads you to a hidden phone. It uses precise distance on iPhones that support it, and Bluetooth signal strength otherwise.
Split ClueEach teammate sees a different part of the clue, so you have to talk. In Solo Mode you see every part.
Meta PuzzleThe finale: it uses answers from earlier puzzles.

Visual and motion puzzles have alternatives for VoiceOver and other needs. See Accessibility.

13. Tag Workshop

Home › Tag Workshop. Pick a room at the top; its clues are listed with how many are written.

  • Write: tap a clue, then hold a sticker to the top of your iPhone. A checkmark confirms it, with the chip type.
  • Batch Write: walks you through every clue in the room, one sticker after another.
  • Inspect: shows what’s on any tag: chip, capacity, contents, and which room and clue it belongs to.
  • Test Scan: shows exactly what players will see when they scan a tag.
  • Health Check: scan all of a room’s tags in a row before a game; any missing ones are flagged.
  • Erase: wipes a tag so it reads as blank.
  • Lock: makes a tag permanently read-only. This can’t be undone, and you must type LOCK to confirm. Locked tags can never be rewritten, even for another room.
  • QR code: open a clue to show or share its QR code.

Small stickers: NTAG213 stickers can’t hold everything, so EscapOtap writes a shorter link-only version and tells you. It plays exactly the same.

Tags are signed for their room, so players can’t fake one, and a tag from one room never opens another.

14. Print Kit

Home › Print Kit. Pick a room, the paper size (US Letter or A4) and the sections you want, then tap Create PDF. Preview it, then Print with AirPrint or Share it (for example, Save to Files).

  • Case file cover: a dossier-style cover page.
  • Clue cards: six per page, each with the room’s art, a QR code and a circle where an NFC sticker can go. Red herrings look just like real clues.
  • GM cheat sheet: where each clue goes, the full walkthrough with every answer and hint, and a reset checklist.
  • Handouts: cut-out cipher wheels and keys, logic grid sheets, split-clue cards and a Morse chart, depending on the room.

Print at 100% scale (not “fit to page”) so cipher wheels line up and stickers fit their circles.

15. Rooms Library

Home › Rooms Library shows every room on your iPhone, on four shelves: All, Built-in, Mine and Imported.

  • Search by title, theme, author or room code.
  • Sort and filter by name, recently added, difficulty or length, or show favorites only.
  • Touch and hold a room for shortcuts: favorite, Play Solo, Host, Print Kit, Export.
  • Import an .escapotap room file from the toolbar.

Tap a room to open its page: its story, Play Solo and Host a Game, your records, and Get It Ready (tags written, Write Tags, Check Tags, Print Kit). The star marks it as a favorite, and the ⋯ menu has Edit, Playtest, Duplicate, Remix, Export and Delete (some options only apply to your own rooms).

16. Room Builder

Home › Room Builder › New Room. A four-step wizard sets the name and theme, players and time limit, the opening story, and whether to start blank or with a Starter Kit (a note, a keypad lock and an exit, ready to play).

The editor

  • Details & Story: cover photo, players and time, how the room is won, and the opening, escape and time’s-up text.
  • Puzzles: add any of the 21 types. Each has its own form and a Preview that plays it exactly as players will. Ciphers encode as you type, word locks can fit their wheels to the answer, and patterns, mazes, hotspots and logic grids are drawn on screen. Set accepted answers, three hints, what solving it gives (items, clues, a story moment, bonus time), and what it waits for (Opens After).
  • Puzzle Flow: a diagram of what opens what. Loops show in red, and puzzles a test run couldn’t reach show in orange.
  • Tags: each clue’s role, what it links to, its label, and a photo of where it’s hidden (very handy when resetting).
  • Items & Clues: items with icons and combinations, and clues with optional photos.
  • Events: timed or triggered blackouts, sounds, story messages and bonuses.
  • Story Moments and General Hints.

Changes save automatically.

Check, test and share

  • Check Room lists problems and suggestions (tap one to jump to it), plays your room in the background with the right answers to prove it can be won, and estimates its difficulty and length.
  • Playtest with Use Virtual Tags on lets you play before writing a single sticker.
  • Write Tags opens Batch Write for the room.
  • Export .escapotap File shares the room, with its photos and recordings, by AirDrop, Messages or Files. Opening the file on another iPhone imports it. If you already have that room, choose Replace My Copy or Keep Both.

Built-in rooms can’t be edited, but Remix makes your own copy with a new title, theme and length. Duplicate copies one of your rooms. Copies always get their own room code, so tags written for one room never open another.

For free, you can keep 2 rooms of your own (created, duplicated or remixed). Imported rooms don’t count. EscapOtap Pro removes the limit and lets you add the advanced puzzle types marked PRO.

18. Siri, Lock Screen and more

  • Lock Screen and Dynamic Island: during a game, the time left and current objective appear on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. Turn this off in Settings › Display › Lock Screen timer.
  • Siri and Shortcuts: say “Start the EscapOtap tutorial”, “Play The Haunted Manor in EscapOtap”, “Host The Vault Heist in EscapOtap” or “Scan a clue with EscapOtap”. The same actions are in the Shortcuts app.
  • Control Center: add EscapOtap’s Scan a Clue control to Control Center or the Lock Screen. In a game it opens the scanner; otherwise it opens Inspect Tag.
  • Spotlight: search for a room’s name from the Home Screen to open its page.

19. Settings

Player Profile

Your name and avatar, shown to others in hosted games.

EscapOtap Pro & Purchases

Your plan, theme packs you own, your room count, Restore Purchases, and Manage Subscription for the yearly plan.

Game

  • Timer: default length (15 to 120 minutes) and whether the clock counts down or up.
  • Hints: hint cost (free, time penalty, points, or disabled), the penalty per hint, and Solo auto-hints with how long before one is offered.
  • Penalties: wrong answer cost (none, 10 seconds, 30 seconds or 1 minute).
  • Multiplayer: allow late join and team switching by default.
  • Difficulty: Easy (extra hints, highlighted clues, gentler penalties), Normal, or Hard (no item descriptions, doubled penalties).
  • Atmosphere: scare level (Kid-friendly to Terrifying) and Family-friendly mode, which removes jump scares, alarms, blackouts and spooky voices.

Settings are fixed when a game starts, so changing them mid-game has no effect on that game.

Audio & Haptics

Music, effects and voice volumes, haptic strength (off, subtle, strong), and the ambient soundscape. Game sounds follow your iPhone’s silent switch.

Display

Appearance (Automatic, Noir, Light, or Match Room Theme, which re-skins each room), accent color, Large timer, Lock Screen timer, Reduce motion and Reduce flashing.

Accessibility

Colorblind-safe palette, accessible Morse and flash, alternatives for visual puzzles, and extra time for timed puzzles. See Accessibility.

Privacy & Data

The privacy policy, the Game Center switch, Export My Data (a file with your settings, history and rooms) and Clear Game History.

About

The app version, this User Manual, Contact Support and font credits.

21. Accessibility

  • VoiceOver works on every screen. Wheels, dials and the maze can be adjusted by swiping up or down.
  • Visual puzzles offer alternatives: a list of hotspots for Image Inspect, “Assemble For Me” for jigsaws, Morse as text, and transcripts for audio clues. Turn on Settings › Accessibility › Alternatives for visual puzzles and Accessible Morse & flash.
  • Timed puzzle time gives 1.5×, 2× or 3× longer on timed puzzles.
  • Colorblind-safe palette uses blue and orange, and every status has its own symbol.
  • Dynamic Type: text follows your iPhone’s text size, up to the largest accessibility sizes.
  • Reduce flashing turns off blackout flicker, strobes and glitch effects, and keeps Morse off the flashlight. Reduce motion is followed too.
  • The tilt maze has arrow buttons, and Morse clues fall back to the screen if the flashlight or vibration isn’t available.

22. Game night tips

  • Start with the tutorial, even for experienced players. It takes 5 minutes and teaches scanning.
  • Set up while players are out of the room, and run a Health Check before you start.
  • Hide clues at hand height, never anywhere unsafe, high, or that needs moving heavy furniture. Tell players what’s off-limits.
  • Charge every phone: screens stay on during games.
  • Teams of 2 to 4 work best. With more players, split into competing teams.
  • As Game Master, be generous with hints early on. A stuck team has less fun than a fast one.
  • Reset quickly with the cheat sheet’s reset checklist, and the hiding-spot photos for your own rooms.

23. Troubleshooting

The tag won’t scan

Hold the top edge of the iPhone flat to the sticker for a second or two. Thick cases or metal behind the sticker can block NFC. Scanning the same tag again within 3 seconds is ignored, and many fast scans start a short cooldown. Check the tag with Tag Workshop › Inspect, rewrite it if needed, or use its QR card.

“Counterfeit” or “this tag belongs to another room”

The tag was written for a different room, or by an older version of the app. Rewrite it in the Tag Workshop for the room you’re playing.

Players can’t find or join the game

Everyone needs the same version of EscapOtap, Wi-Fi and Bluetooth on, and Local Network access allowed (iPhone Settings › Apps › EscapOtap). Make sure the code is right, and that the game hasn’t started with late joining turned off.

A player keeps disconnecting

Keep EscapOtap open on screen: locking the phone or switching apps pauses the connection. It reconnects by itself, or tap Rejoin on Home after reopening the app.

No sound, or audio clues are silent

Turn off silent mode and raise the volume. Check the sliders in Settings › Audio & Haptics.

A purchase isn’t showing

Tap Restore Purchases in Settings › EscapOtap Pro & Purchases, signed in with the Apple Account you bought with.

The proximity radar says it needs a second phone

Proximity Hunt tracks another phone, so it only works in hosted games.
