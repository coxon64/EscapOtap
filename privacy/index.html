Privacy Policy

Your game stays yours

Effective 2 October 2026 · Applies to the EscapOtap app for iPhone

The short version

EscapOtap doesn’t collect any personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and the app has no servers. What you create and play stays on your iPhone unless you choose to share it.

Who we are

EscapOtap is made by Mike Coxon, an independent developer. For any privacy question or request, email cawingcrows.music@gmail.com.

What’s stored on your iPhone

EscapOtap saves the following on your iPhone only. The developer never receives it.

  • Rooms you build or import, including photos of hiding spots and voice recordings you add.
  • Your game history, best times, awards, and saved games in progress.
  • Your settings, player name and avatar.
  • Journal notes and sketches made during a game (deleted automatically after 7 days).

Deleting EscapOtap deletes all of it. In the app, Settings › Privacy & Data lets you export your data as a file or clear your game history.

Playing together

Group games connect nearby iPhones directly to each other over encrypted Bluetooth and Wi-Fi (Apple’s MultipeerConnectivity), without any server. Your player name, avatar, chat messages and game progress are shared only with the phones in that game, and only while it’s running. Custom rooms, including their photos and recordings, are sent to players’ phones for the game and deleted from them when they leave, unless they already had the room.

Sharing rooms and results

When you export a room as an .escapotap file, share a result card, or export your data, the file goes wherever you send it (AirDrop, Messages, Files and so on). EscapOtap doesn’t keep a copy anywhere else.

Game Center (optional)

Game Center is off unless you turn it on in Settings › Privacy & Data or the Hall of Fame. When it’s on, your best times in the built-in rooms and your achievements are sent to Apple’s Game Center, where your Game Center friends can see them. Apple handles that data under Apple’s Privacy Policy. Turn it off at any time to stop sending anything.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple. EscapOtap only learns which items you own, so it can unlock them. It never sees your name, Apple Account or payment details.

Permissions

PermissionWhat it’s used for
NFCReading and writing EscapOtap clue tags. It reads only the tags you hold to your iPhone while scanning, and never reads payment cards.
CameraScanning QR clue cards and join codes, and photographing hiding spots in the Room Builder.
MicrophoneRecording voice clues you add in the Room Builder.
Local Network and BluetoothConnecting players’ phones to the Game Master.
Nearby InteractionMeasuring the distance between phones for proximity puzzles.

Each permission is asked for only when a feature first needs it, and everything captured stays on your devices.

What we don’t do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or third-party software development kits.
  • No tracking across apps or websites.
  • No selling or sharing of data, because none is collected.

If you’ve chosen in iOS Settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may send the developer anonymous crash reports. That’s controlled by you in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements on your iPhone.

Children

Because EscapOtap collects no personal data, it collects none from children either. Family-friendly mode in Settings › Game removes jump scares, alarms, blackouts and spooky voices.

Your choices

Since your data lives only on your iPhone, you’re always in control: export it, clear your history, turn Game Center off, or delete the app. If you have a question or request about your privacy, email cawingcrows.music@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date and will ship in an app update.
