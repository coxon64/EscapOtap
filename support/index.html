Stuck? We’ll get you out.

Answers to common questions, and how to reach a real person.

Help us help you

When reporting a problem, please include:

  • Your iPhone model and iOS version (Settings › General › About on your iPhone).
  • The EscapOtap version, shown at the bottom of EscapOtap’s Settings.
  • The room you were playing, and whether it was Solo, hosting or joining.
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened. A screenshot or screen recording helps a lot.
  • For tag problems: the sticker type (for example NTAG215) and what Tag Workshop › Inspect shows.

Frequently asked questions

Getting started

Do I need NFC stickers?

No. Every clue has a QR version. Open Print Kit, choose a room and print the clue cards, then use the QR button next to SCAN during a game. To try a room with no setup at all, open it in the Rooms Library and choose ⋯ › Playtest.

Which NFC stickers should I buy?

NTAG215 stickers are the best choice. NTAG213 and NTAG216 work too (NTAG213 is small, so EscapOtap writes a shorter link-only version). For metal surfaces, buy “anti-metal” tags. MIFARE Classic tags can’t be used with iPhone.

Does every player need the app?

In a hosted game, each player uses their own iPhone with EscapOtap installed. Players never need to buy anything: only the Game Master needs to own the room. In Solo Mode, one iPhone is enough for the whole group.

Do we need Wi-Fi or internet?

No internet is needed. Phones talk to each other directly, so just keep Wi-Fi and Bluetooth turned on (in Control Center or Settings) on every phone. They don’t need to join the same network.

Scanning

My iPhone won’t read a tag
  • Hold the top edge of the iPhone flat against the sticker for a second or two. Thick cases and metal behind the sticker can block NFC.
  • Make sure the tag was written for this room: Tag Workshop › Inspect shows what’s on it.
  • Scanning the same tag again within 3 seconds is ignored, and lots of fast scans start a short “too fast” cooldown. Wait a moment and try again.
  • If a tag still fails, rewrite it in the Tag Workshop or use its QR card.
A tag says “Counterfeit”, “another room” or “not an EscapOtap tag”

Each room signs its own tags. A tag written for a different room, an older version of the app, or another app won’t open this room. Rewrite it with Tag Workshop › Write for the room you’re playing. Tags written before the app was renamed EscapOtap need rewriting too.

The QR button asks for the camera

QR scanning needs camera access. If you said no earlier, turn it on in your iPhone’s Settings › Apps › EscapOtap › Camera.

Multiplayer

Players can’t find my game
  • Everyone needs the same version of EscapOtap. Update from the App Store if anyone is behind.
  • Allow Local Network access when asked. If you said no, turn it on in your iPhone’s Settings › Apps › EscapOtap › Local Network (or Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network).
  • Keep Wi-Fi and Bluetooth on, and stay within the same home.
  • Players can type the 4-digit code, tap the game under Nearby Games, or scan the Game Master’s QR code with the Camera app.
A player keeps dropping out

Locking a phone or switching to another app pauses its connection. EscapOtap keeps the screen awake during games and reconnects by itself when the player comes back. If the app was closed, reopen it and tap Rejoin on the Home screen. If the Game Master’s app closed, they can tap Host a Game › Resume.

“Game Full” or “Game already started”

A game holds up to 8 players plus the Game Master. To let people join after the start, the Game Master turns on Allow late joining when setting up the game.

Sound and effects

There’s no sound

EscapOtap’s sounds follow your iPhone’s ring/silent switch, so turn silent mode off and raise the volume. Check Settings › Audio & Haptics in EscapOtap for the music, effects and voice sliders.

Flashing effects are uncomfortable

Turn on Settings › Display › Reduce flashing. Blackout flicker, alarm strobes and glitch effects stop, and Morse clues use a gentle on-screen lamp instead of the flashlight. Family-friendly mode in Settings › Game also removes scares.

Purchases

I bought something but it’s still locked

Open Settings › EscapOtap Pro & Purchases and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you’re signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with. Family Sharing members get shared purchases automatically.

How do I cancel EscapOtap Pro (Yearly)?

Open your iPhone’s Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions, choose EscapOtap Pro and tap Cancel Subscription, at least 24 hours before it renews. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for. You can also tap Manage Subscription in EscapOtap’s Settings.

How do I ask for a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose the purchase.

Your data

How do I move my rooms to a new iPhone?

Rooms you build can be exported one at a time: open the room in the Room Builder or Rooms Library and choose Export .escapotap File, then AirDrop it to the new iPhone. Purchases come back with Restore Purchases.

How do I delete my data?

Everything is stored only on your iPhone. Settings › Privacy & Data › Clear Game History removes your history, and deleting the app removes everything. See the privacy policy.
